Near-neutral colors — whites, grays, and blacks — are typically absent from generated palettes. Vibrant.js uses Median-Cut Quantization to identify perceptually distinct, saturated color regions; near-zero-saturation tones carry minimal chromatic information and are deprioritized in favor of hues that meaningfully define the image’s visual character.
